Kenyan Tax Information

PAYE Tax Bands

PAYE (Pay As You Earn) is the income tax deducted monthly based on your taxable salary and the applicable tax band.

  • 0 – 24,000 → 10%
  • 24,001 – 32,333 → 25%
  • 32,334 – 500,000 → 30%
  • 500,001 – 800,000 → 32.5%
  • Above 800,000 → 35%
    •
  • Minimum Taxable Income: KES 24,001.00 per month

NHIF / SHA Information

NHIF is transitioning toward SHIF (Social Health Insurance Fund), which manages the new SHIF contributions for universal health coverage in Kenya.

NHDF (Housing Levy)

The National Housing Development Fund (NHDF) supports affordable housing initiatives through a mandatory employee contribution of 1.5% of the gross salary.

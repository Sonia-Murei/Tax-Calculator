Kenyan Tax Information

PAYE Tax Bands

  • 0 – 24,000 → 10%
  • 24,001 – 32,333 → 25%
  • 32,334 – 500,000 → 30%
  • 500,001 – 800,000 → 32.5%
  • Above 800,000 → 35%
  • Minimum Taxable Income: KES 24,001.00 per month
